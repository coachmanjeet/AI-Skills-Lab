# TripCo facts (fictional example)

TripCo is a fictional travel company. Every number below is made up for this lab. Do not quote any of it as real.

- The TripCo trip-change agent handles 58% of change requests end to end.
- 31% of fare-difference sessions end in a handoff to a human.
- Humans rewrite 44% of multi-city itineraries.
- Full refund window: within 24 hours of booking. After 24 hours, the traveler gets a fare-difference quote first.
- Any reissue that requires payment needs explicit human approval. No exceptions.
